# Desktop Companion roadmap

This is the user-provided phase order.

1. Board setup and hardware verification: USB programming, Flash, PSRAM, Serial Monitor.
2. LCD and touchscreen: display driver, touch calibration and orientation.
3. Animated facial expressions: eyes, blinking, moods and smooth transitions.
4. RGB LED ring: lighting synchronized with facial expressions.
5. microSD and sound: store assets and play sound effects.
6. YTMDesktop integration: desktop connection, music controls and track metadata.
7. Microphone and speaker: recording, playback and audio routing.
8. AI assistant: cloud conversation, speech recognition and spoken responses.
9. Alarms and RTC: alarm scheduler, notifications and touch controls.
10. Integration and enclosure: combine services, improve reliability and design housing.
